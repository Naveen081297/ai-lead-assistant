# Certification and Assessment

## How you are graded

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Weekly assignments | 30% |
| Module quizzes | 20% |
| Capstone project | 50% |

- Pass: 60% overall and at least 50% on the capstone
- Pass with distinction: 85% overall

## Capstone retakes

If you fail the capstone, you can resubmit once within 30 days at no cost. A second resubmission costs INR 2,000.

## Certificate

- Digital certificate with a QR code that employers can scan to verify it
- Issued within 15 working days of your final result
- A printed copy can be couriered within India for INR 300

## Is the certificate accredited?

Brightwater certificates are industry certificates issued by the academy. They are not university degrees and are not accredited by UGC or AICTE.

## Python Foundations

Python Foundations has no capstone. You get a certificate of completion when you finish all lessons and pass the final quiz with 60% or more.
