# Digital Marketing Accelerator

A practical 12-week program covering paid ads, SEO, social media and marketing analytics.

## Who it is for

Anyone aged 18 or over who has passed Class 12. Suited to students, business owners and marketing executives. No aptitude test is required.

## Format and schedule

- Duration: 12 weeks
- Schedule: weekday mornings, Tuesday and Thursday, 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM IST, plus one recorded module per week
- Mode: live online only
- New batches start on the first Monday of every month

## Curriculum

1. Marketing fundamentals and customer journeys
2. Search engine optimisation (on-page, technical, local SEO)
3. Google Ads: search, display and Performance Max campaigns
4. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)
5. Email marketing and marketing automation, including CRM-based lead nurturing
6. Content and social media strategy
7. Google Analytics 4 and reporting
8. Final project: plan and run a live campaign with a INR 2,000 ad budget provided by the academy

## Certifications

Learners are guided through the free Google Ads and Google Analytics certifications as part of the course.

## Fee

INR 45,000 plus 18% GST.
