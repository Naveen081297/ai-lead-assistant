# Fee Sheet 2027

Fees apply to all batches starting between 1 January 2027 and 31 December 2027. All amounts are in Indian rupees (INR).

## Program fees

| Program | Course fee | GST (18%) | Total payable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Analytics Pro | 89,000 | 16,020 | 1,05,020 |
| Full Stack Web Development | 1,24,000 | 22,320 | 1,46,320 |
| Digital Marketing Accelerator | 45,000 | 8,100 | 53,100 |
| UI/UX Design Professional | 79,000 | 14,220 | 93,220 |
| Python Foundations | 18,500 | 3,330 | 21,830 |

## Registration fee

A registration fee of INR 5,000 is paid to reserve a seat. It is adjusted against the course fee, so it is not an extra cost. The registration fee is non-refundable.

## Payment options

- **Full payment:** pay the full fee before the batch starts and get 5% off the course fee.
- **Three instalments:** 40% before the batch starts, 30% by the end of month 2, 30% by the end of month 4. Available for Data Analytics Pro, Full Stack Web Development and UI/UX Design Professional.
- **No-cost EMI:** 6 or 9 monthly instalments through our lending partner, for course fees above INR 40,000. Subject to the partner's credit approval. No interest is charged to the learner.

Python Foundations must be paid in full at enrolment.

## Payment methods

UPI, debit or credit card, net banking and NEFT/RTGS bank transfer. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## Late payment

Instalments paid more than 7 days late attract a late fee of INR 500. Access to the learning platform is paused if an instalment is more than 21 days overdue.
