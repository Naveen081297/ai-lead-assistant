# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to all programs except where stated.

## Cancelling before the batch starts

If you cancel at least 7 days before your batch start date, you get a full refund of everything you paid except the INR 5,000 registration fee.

If you cancel less than 7 days before the start date, you get a refund of 75% of the course fee paid, minus the registration fee.

## Cancelling after the batch starts

- Within the first 14 days of the batch: 50% of the course fee paid is refunded.
- After 14 days: no refund.

## Python Foundations

Python Foundations has a 7-day refund window from enrolment, provided you have watched less than 20% of the course content. After that, no refund.

## Batch transfer instead of a refund

You can move to a later batch of the same program once, free of charge, if you ask within 60 days of your original start date. A second transfer costs INR 3,000.

## Switching programs

You can switch to a different program within the first 14 days of your batch. You pay the difference if the new program costs more. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days of approval. GST paid is refunded in proportion to the course fee refunded. For no-cost EMI enrolments, the refund is paid to the lending partner and the loan is closed or reduced.

## How to request

Email support@brightwater-academy.example with your enrolment ID. Requests are acknowledged within 2 working days.
