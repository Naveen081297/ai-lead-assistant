# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?

Yes. A free live demo class runs every Saturday at 11:00 AM IST. Register through the website or ask your counsellor for the link.

## Can I work full-time while studying?

Yes. Data Analytics Pro and UI/UX Design Professional run on weekends, and Full Stack Web Development runs on weekday evenings, so they are designed for working professionals.

## Do you offer education loans?

We do not lend money ourselves. No-cost EMI is available through our lending partner for course fees above INR 40,000 (see the fee sheet). You can also take an education loan from any bank; we provide a fee letter on request.

## Do you provide hostel accommodation?

No. For the Pune campus we share a list of paying-guest accommodations nearby, but we have no tie-up with them and do not take responsibility for them.

## Is there an age limit?

There is no upper age limit. The minimum age is 18, or 16 for Python Foundations.

## Can companies train their teams with you?

Yes. We run customised corporate training. Contact corporate@brightwater-academy.example.

## What language are classes taught in?

All classes are taught in English. Mentors can explain doubts in Hindi or Marathi if you ask.

## Can I pause my course?

Yes, once, for up to 3 months, for medical or work reasons. You re-join a later batch at the same point in the curriculum. Ask through support.

## Do you have a laptop rental option?

No. You need your own laptop. See the technical requirements.
