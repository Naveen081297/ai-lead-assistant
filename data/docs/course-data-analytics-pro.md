# Data Analytics Pro

Become a job-ready data analyst in 24 weeks.

## Who it is for

Graduates and final-year students from any discipline, and working professionals moving into analytics. No coding experience is required, but learners must pass the aptitude test (see the admissions process).

## Format and schedule

- Duration: 24 weeks (about 6 months)
- Schedule: weekend batches, Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM IST
- Mode: live online, or classroom at the Pune campus
- Effort: about 6 hours of live class plus 6 hours of self-study per week

## Curriculum

1. Excel for analysis: lookups, pivot tables, dashboards (weeks 1-3)
2. SQL with PostgreSQL: joins, window functions, query tuning (weeks 4-8)
3. Python for data: pandas, NumPy, data cleaning (weeks 9-14)
4. Statistics and A/B testing (weeks 15-17)
5. Power BI: data modelling, DAX, report design (weeks 18-21)
6. Capstone project on a real-world dataset (weeks 22-24)

## Tools you will use

Excel, PostgreSQL, Python (Jupyter), Power BI Desktop. All software used is free or has a free tier.

## Fee

INR 89,000 plus 18% GST. See the fee sheet for payment plans.

## Outcome

Learners who complete the capstone receive the Data Analytics Pro certificate and are eligible for placement assistance.
