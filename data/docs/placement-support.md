# Placement Support

Brightwater offers placement **assistance**. We do not guarantee a job.

## Who is eligible

Learners in Data Analytics Pro, Full Stack Web Development and UI/UX Design Professional who:

- attend at least 80% of live sessions, and
- pass the capstone project.

Digital Marketing Accelerator and Python Foundations learners get the resume review and the job board, but not mock interviews or referrals.

## What you get

- Resume and LinkedIn profile review
- Three mock interviews (one HR, two technical) with feedback
- Access to the Brightwater job board
- Referrals to our hiring partner network of more than 120 companies
- Career support for 12 months after course completion

## Results (batches completing July 2025 to June 2026)

| Program | Eligible learners placed within 6 months | Median starting salary |
|---|---|---|
| Data Analytics Pro | 78% | INR 5.2 lakh per year |
| Full Stack Web Development | 74% | INR 6.0 lakh per year |
| UI/UX Design Professional | 69% | INR 5.5 lakh per year |

Figures cover only learners who were eligible for placement support and actively looking for a job. They were reviewed by an independent auditor.

## Internships

Final-month learners can apply for unpaid or stipend-based internships posted on the job board. The academy does not run internships itself.
