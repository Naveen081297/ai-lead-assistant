# Online Learning and Technical Requirements

## Learning platform

All learners, online and classroom, use **Brightwater Learn**, our learning platform, for class links, recordings, assignments and quizzes. It works in any modern browser and has Android and iOS apps.

## Live classes and recordings

- Live online classes run on Zoom. Links appear in Brightwater Learn.
- Every live class is recorded. Recordings are available within 24 hours.
- Recordings stay accessible for 12 months after your course ends.

## Attendance

You must attend at least 80% of live sessions to be eligible for placement support. Watching a recording does not count as attendance. If you miss a class, you can join the same session in a parallel batch, where one exists.

## Computer requirements

- Laptop or desktop (a tablet or phone is not enough)
- Minimum 8 GB RAM; 16 GB recommended for Full Stack Web Development and Data Analytics Pro
- Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or better, or an Apple M-series Mac
- Windows 10 or later, macOS 12 or later, or Ubuntu 22.04 or later
- Webcam and headset with microphone

## Internet

A stable connection of at least 10 Mbps is recommended. Your camera must be on during assessments and mock interviews.

## Technical help

Email support@brightwater-academy.example. Support replies within 4 working hours, Monday to Saturday.
