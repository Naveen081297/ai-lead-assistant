# UI/UX Design Professional

Learn user research, interaction design and visual design, and graduate with a portfolio.

## Who it is for

Graduates and final-year students from any background, including non-design backgrounds. Instead of the aptitude test, applicants complete a short design exercise (about 2 hours, done at home).

## Format and schedule

- Duration: 20 weeks
- Schedule: weekend batches, Saturday and Sunday, 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM IST
- Mode: live online, or classroom at the Bengaluru centre
- Effort: 6 hours of live class plus 5 to 8 hours of project work per week

## Curriculum

1. Design thinking and user research methods
2. Information architecture and user flows
3. Wireframing and prototyping in Figma
4. Visual design: typography, colour, layout, design systems
5. Usability testing and accessibility (WCAG basics)
6. Portfolio and case study writing

## Portfolio

Every learner finishes with three portfolio case studies: two guided projects and one independent project reviewed by an industry mentor.

## Software

Figma (free education plan). A laptop with at least 8 GB RAM is enough for this program.
