# About Brightwater Digital Academy

Brightwater Digital Academy is a career-focused training institute for technology and digital skills. It was founded in 2016 and has trained more than 9,000 learners.

## Campuses

- **Pune campus:** 4th Floor, Riverside Tech Park, Baner, Pune 411045. This is the main campus and hosts the admissions office.
- **Bengaluru centre:** 2nd Floor, Lakeview Plaza, Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru 560095. Classroom batches for Full Stack Web Development and UI/UX Design only.

All programs are also available as live online batches.

## Office hours

The admissions office is open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST. It is closed on Sundays and national holidays.
