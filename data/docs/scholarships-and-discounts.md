# Scholarships and Discounts

## Women in Tech scholarship

Women applicants get 20% off the course fee for Data Analytics Pro and Full Stack Web Development. Limited to 10 seats per batch, allotted in order of registration.

## Merit scholarship

Applicants who score 85% or more on the aptitude test get 15% off the course fee. Applies to Data Analytics Pro and Full Stack Web Development.

## Early bird discount

Register at least 30 days before the batch start date and get 10% off the course fee. Available on all programs except Python Foundations.

## Referral reward

If an alumnus or current learner refers you and you enrol, you get INR 5,000 off your course fee and the referrer also receives INR 5,000.

## Upfront payment discount

5% off the course fee if paid in full before the batch starts (see the fee sheet).

## Stacking rules

- Only one of these can be used per enrolment: Women in Tech, merit scholarship, early bird.
- The referral reward and the upfront payment discount can be added on top of any one of them.
- Discounts apply to the course fee before GST.
- The registration fee is never discounted.

## How to apply

Mention the scholarship or discount during your counselling call. Supporting proof (for example, aptitude test score) is checked before the discount is applied to your invoice.
